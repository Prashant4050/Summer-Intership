About Student Management System

A Student Management System (SMS) is a software application designed to simplify the management of student information in schools, colleges, and universities. It helps administrators maintain accurate records while reducing paperwork and manual effort.

Objectives

  • Maintain student information digitally.
  • Reduce paperwork and manual data entry.
  • Provide quick access to student records.
  • Improve accuracy of stored information.
  • Save time for educational institutions.
